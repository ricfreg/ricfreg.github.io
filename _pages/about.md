<p style="text-align: justify;">
  I am a Full Professor of Economics at the Universidade Federal de Juiz de Fora (UFJF), Brazil, and former Vice President of the Brazilian Econometric Society (SBE).
</p>

<p style="text-align: justify;">
  I hold a PhD in Economics from the University of São Paulo (USP) with a doctoral stay at the University of Illinois. My research agenda centers on Labor Economics, Education, and Inequality, with a particular focus on the evaluation of public policies using econometric methods and causal inference.
</p>
