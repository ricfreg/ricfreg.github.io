## Current Research Projects

### 1. Effects of Forced Immigration on the Brazilian Labor Market (2026-Present)
Investigation of the integration and performance of forcibly displaced migrants (particularly Venezuelan migrants) in the formal Brazilian labor market, examining firm hiring behavior, career trajectories, and wage effects.

### 2. Inflation and Wage Inequality in Brazil (2024-Present)
Analysis of the relationship between inflation and wage inequality in the Brazilian labor market.

### 3. Higher Education and Labor Market Outcomes in Brazil (2019-Present)
Identification of the impact of Brazilian higher education on labor market outcomes.

### 4. Networks in the Labor Market: Dynamics, Allocative Inefficiency, and Employment Outcomes (2019-Present)
Analysis of the dynamics of professional networks and their effects on skill-job matching and employment outcomes.

### 5. Wage Differentials and Worker Skills in Brazil (2019-Present)
Identification of wage premiums resulting from worker skills.

### 6. Gender and the Labor Market in Brazil (2017-Present)
Diagnostic analysis of women's situation in the Brazilian labor market, including investigation of maternity leave effects and employer discrimination.
